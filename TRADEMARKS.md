This policy explains what you may and may not do with Aria's marks even when
you are free to use the code.

This policy covers:

- The wordmarks **Aria** and **Aria Builder**

## Permitted Use (No Permission Required)

You may use the marks **only** for accurate, nominative reference — to
identify the source of the software, not to brand your own product:

- Truthfully describe that software is built on, forked from, derived from,
  or compatible with Aria or Aria Builder. Examples:
  - *Acme CMS — a fork of Aria Builder*
  - *Built with Aria Builder*
  - *Compatible with Aria's storage adapter*
- Link to the official repository or [ariabuilder.io](https://ariabuilder.io)
  when attributing upstream
- Mention Aria in changelogs, articles, talks, or comparisons that describe
  the real relationship between your work and this project

Nominative use must not suggest endorsement, sponsorship, or official
affiliation with Statice Origins Inc.

## Prohibited Use (Without Prior Written Permission)

You may **not**:

- Distribute a **modified** fork, deployment, or hosted service under the
  name **Aria**, **Aria Builder**, or any confusingly similar variant
  (`Aria Plus`, `Aria Enterprise`, `MyAria`, `aria-yourcompany`, and the
  like)
- Use Aria logos or wordmarks as **your** product brand — in README heroes,
  marketing sites, npm display names, SaaS landing pages, or a rebranded
  `/admin` experience that presents your build as official Aria
- Register domain names, package names, social handles, or company names
  whose primary purpose is to trade on Aria's identity or imply official
  status
- State or imply that Statice Origins Inc., Aria Builder, or ariabuilder.io
  endorses, sponsors, or is affiliated with your fork or service

### Examples

| Use | Allowed? |

## Unmodified Redistribution

If you redistribute **substantially unmodified** source or binaries under the
Apache License, Version 2.0 and keep the required notices intact, you may
continue to refer to that copy as **Aria** or **Aria Builder** when
describing the upstream software you are distributing — for example, a mirror
or vendor package of the official release.

## Modified Forks and Deployments

If you fork Aria, customize it, or run a materially different deployment:

1. **Rename** your product. Choose a name that does not include "Aria" or
   suggest you are the official project.
2. **Document provenance** — e.g. *Forked from [Aria Builder](https://github.com/...)*.
3. **Do not** present your build as "Aria" or use Aria branding as if it
   were your own, unless you are distributing an official, unmodified
   release from Statice Origins Inc.

The Apache License, Version 2.0 guarantees you may fork, modify, and ship the code. This
policy guarantees the **name** remains a reliable signal of origin.

## Logo Use

Use of Aria logos and design marks requires **prior written permission**
except when accurately attributing the **official** upstream project in
documentation (and making clear the project is official Aria, not your fork).

To request permission for logo or extended brand use, email
**brand@ariabuilder.io** with:

- Who you are and what you want to call your project or service
- How Aria marks would appear (name, logo placement, context)
- The relationship between your work and upstream Aria
